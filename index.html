English Vocabulary

Improve

Meaning: To make something better.

Example: I want to improve my communication skills.

Confident

Meaning: Feeling sure about yourself.

Example: I want to become a confident speaker.

Express

Meaning: To communicate an idea or feeling.

Example: I can express my thoughts clearly.

Consistent

Meaning: Doing something regularly.

Example: Consistent practice helps me improve.

Opportunity

Meaning: A chance to do something.
